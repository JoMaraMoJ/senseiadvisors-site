The problem

Where manufacturing accounting breaks.

Manufacturing numbers look solid from the outside — parts, pallets, shipments. Inside the ledger, they break in predictable places, and each one surfaces at the worst time: a pricing decision, a lender review, a count that doesn't tie.

Standard costs nobody believes

Standards set once and never maintained. Every margin discussion becomes an argument about whose numbers are right.

One lump called “variance”

Price, usage, rate, efficiency, and overhead blended into a single unexplained number. Nothing to fix, because nothing is identified.

Inventory that doesn't tie

Raw, WIP, and finished goods disagree with the floor. Counts become crises instead of confirmations.

Backlog and cut-off

Shipments and revenue recognized on different calendars. The backlog roll is a guess, and month-end revenue moves with it.

Product-line margins too late

Which lines make money is learned after the quarter — if ever. Pricing and capacity decisions run on stale numbers.

Working capital invisible

The cash conversion cycle and inventory days are never computed from the close. Cash sits in inventory, and nobody can say how much.

The system

Built once, for manufacturing. Run every month.

Sensei FinanceOS for Manufacturing is a complete close system — revenue, inventory, cost, and reporting — installed on your existing accounting system, whatever that system is.

02

Inventory & COGS

Raw, WIP, and finished goods on rollforwards that tie to the floor — with count readiness built in, not bolted on before a count.

03

Standard cost & the five variances

Material price, material usage, labor rate, labor efficiency, and overhead — decomposed and tied to actual minus standard at Gate G2.

04

Product-line P&L, margin & capacity

Line margins and capacity utilization from the same close — not a separate spreadsheet built weeks later.

05

Production labor & payroll

Labor tied to production, not just to payroll registers — so labor cost lands where the work happened.

06

Treasury & working capital

Cash conversion cycle and days inventory recomputed at Gate G3, from closed books — decision-ready because the statements already tie.
