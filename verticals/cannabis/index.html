The problem

Where cannabis accounting breaks.

Licensed cannabis runs on seed-to-sale data, a punitive tax code, and licenses that never stop moving. The accounting breaks in predictable places — and each one surfaces at the worst time: a lender review, a license renewal, a tax filing.

METRC and the books tell two stories

Quantities drift between seed-to-sale and the ledger. Valuation gets built on untied quantities, and nobody finds out until someone asks.

280E reconstructed at tax time

The COGS versus non-deductible split gets invented after the fact — instead of classified as costs post, all year long.

Shrink nobody explains

Inventory losses get treated as a nuisance. Unexplained shrink is a fraud indicator, and it should be handled like one.

Licenses tracked in someone's head

Held, dormant, and renewing licenses across entities — with no summary that ties to the close.

Intercompany between licenses

Transfers and fees between entities that don't eliminate cleanly, month after month.

Cash-heavy operations, light controls

Cash controls that don't match the risk — in the part of the business where the risk is highest.

The system

Built once, for cannabis. Run every month.
