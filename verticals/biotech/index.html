The problem

Where biotech accounting breaks.

Clinical-stage accounting breaks in predictable places — and each one surfaces at the worst time: a board meeting, a fundraise, a diligence request.

Trial accruals built on guesses

CRO and site costs are incurred but not invoiced. The accrual is the key estimate — and the first thing diligence attacks.

Grant money without allowability discipline

Costs get charged to grants that shouldn't be. Deferred and receivable balances don't roll, and nobody can prove what the grant actually funded.

R&D spend nobody can split

Program, platform, and G&A blur into one number. There are no program-level economics — just total spend.

Runway as a rumor

Burn is computed differently every month. The board gets a new definition of runway at every meeting.

Collaboration revenue on a spreadsheet

Milestone and deferred terms live in a spreadsheet, outside the ledger. The revenue follows the spreadsheet, not the agreement.

Prepaids and lab spend drifting

CRO and CMO prepaids are paid up front and consumed without a rollforward. The balance on the books is whatever is left over.

The system

Built once, for biotech. Run every month.

Sensei FinanceOS for Biotech is a complete close system — grants, trial accruals, program economics, and reporting — installed on your existing accounting system, whatever that system is.

01

Grant & collaboration revenue

Deferred and receivable balances roll to $0 unexplained at Gate G1 — and allowability is checked as costs post, not after the grant is spent.

02

Clinical trial accruals

A rollforward by program, trial, and CRO at Gate G2. The estimate is documented — method, inputs, and changes — not improvised at month-end.

03

Program & trial economics

R&D spend by program, tied to the ledger at Gate G3 — so program economics are an output of the close, not a separate analysis.

04

Lab operations & prepaids

CRO and CMO prepaids and lab spend run on rollforwards — paid, consumed, and remaining, proved every month.

05

Cash, runway & investor reporting

Burn and runway computed from closed books — one definition, the same way every month, ready for the board.

06

R&D credits & grant compliance

Credit evidence captured during the year, not reconstructed at tax time — and grant compliance built into an 80-task close.

Audit-ready

Designed to be audited: the PCAOB layer.

Every Sensei FinanceOS build is built to US GAAP, with SEC-reporting discipline, SOX-ready controls, and a PCAOB layer — audit evidence and workpaper discipline built to PCAOB standards.

After the close gates pass, a separate Gate G4 — Audit-Ready — checks evidence, workpapers, and review. A failed G4 blocks the words “audit-ready,” not the statements.
