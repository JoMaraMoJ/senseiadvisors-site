The Seat-Cost Calculator

What does one seat actually cost?

Loaded cost uses 1.25–1.40× the wage — a planning range for payroll taxes, benefits, software seats, and your time managing the seat. Your firm’s actual load differs. The point is the gap, not the decimal.

The wage, as posted

$4,333/mo

Wage × hours × 52 ÷ 12. The number in your head when you post the job.

The loaded seat

$5,417–$6,067/mo

$65,000–$72,800 a year — for one seat, which takes PTO, gets sick, and can resign in your busiest month.

Seat cost per client

$361–$404/client/mo

Before rent, before your review time, before profit. And capacity grows only one way: hire again.

Every new client takes another slice of a seat. When the seat is full, growth waits on recruiting.
