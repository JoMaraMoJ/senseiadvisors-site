Approach

Stabilize. Standardize. Automate.

Automation is downstream of accounting quality. Sensei Advisors first establishes financial integrity, then builds a repeatable operating cadence, and only then automates the work that should be automated.

  1. 01

    Diagnose

    Identify close bottlenecks, unreliable balances, broken handoffs, duplicate work, and reporting gaps.

  2. 02

  3. 03

  4. 04

Audit-ready

Designed to be audited: the PCAOB layer.

Every Sensei FinanceOS build is built to US GAAP, with SEC-reporting discipline, SOX-ready controls, and a PCAOB layer — audit evidence and workpaper discipline built to PCAOB standards.

Gate G4 — Audit-Ready

After the close gates pass, a separate audit-ready gate checks evidence, workpapers, and review. A failed G4 blocks the words “audit-ready,” not the statements.

Evidence & workpapers

Workpapers prepared to be audited: indexed, reviewed, and retained — so an auditor, investor, or acquirer can follow every number back to its support.

Controls that hold

Segregation of duties, fraud and estimate controls, and IT and AI-agent controls are part of the system — not a cleanup project before the audit starts.

International

Built for US and international diligence: the International layer.

FinanceOS is one build with modular activation. Each client picks a US track or an International track — the system underneath is the same. Every standard is anchored two ways: to the FASB Codification and to IFRS Accounting Standards as issued by the IASB. FASB- and IASB-friendly by design, so a diligence team reads the same file on either side of the Atlantic — no translation.

Framework set per entity

Each entity carries its statutory framework, the group reporting framework, and its presentation currency on one register. Nothing is forced into a single framework.

The bridge close

Local statutory close first. Then the framework bridge. Then group consolidation, then the investor pack. The bridge ties with $0 unexplained — or the close does not pass Gate G5.

Instruments read both ways

SAFEs, convertibles, preferred shares, and warrants are analysed under both frameworks before signing — because the same instrument can be equity in one framework and a liability in the other.

Local rules stay local

Statutory calendars, indirect taxes, withholding, and R&D credit evidence are captured in registers, and local advisors file. FinanceOS never replaces the local statutory accountant.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

How long does an install take?

Two to three weeks. Week one maps your chart of accounts, close calendar, and workpapers. Week two builds the system and runs it against last month's actuals — the gates have to pass on real data before go-live. Week three is your first live close, with Sensei reviewing exceptions.

Which accounting systems does Sensei FinanceOS work with?

The one you already run. Sensei FinanceOS installs on your existing accounting system — it does not ask you to migrate ledgers. Connections cover the surrounding stack (payroll, billing, spend, banking); document upload covers the rest. And if you don't have a system yet, I'll recommend one based on your industry and the scope of your needs — that recommendation comes out of the diagnostic, before the install.

How is my financial data protected?

Your documents live in a private client workspace. Access is per client — your files are visible only to you and your Sensei team, never to other clients. System connections are made inside the workspace: sign-in through the provider where that exists, and any API token is stored server-side only, never in your browser or by email. Do not send credentials or sensitive data through the contact form or email — the workspace exists for that.

Who reviews the AI's work?

I do. The major workflows are run by an agent I direct and review; the agent prepares, it does not approve. Judgment, exceptions, and sign-off stay with a person, and a close is not called done until its gates pass and I have reviewed the exceptions.

What do you need from me each month?

Less than you are used to. Connected systems feed most of the close. You provide the documents that never live in the accounting system — through your workspace — answer the items marked Needs you, and approve the reporting pack. One person on your side who can answer questions is enough.

What does it cost?

Fixed scope, fixed price, defined outcome — no hourly billing. The price is set after a short diagnostic, before work starts, so you know the full cost before you commit.

What happens after you reach out?

You send an inquiry. I run a short diagnostic on your books and your systems. Then you get the scope, the price, and the schedule in writing, before work starts — the standard install runs on the two to three weeks above; a complex engagement carries a longer schedule, set in that same plan. Once you approve it, I set up your private workspace, and we start with your first close.
