Services

From cleanup to a finance function that runs.

Engagements are designed around the point of friction: unreliable books, an inconsistent close, limited financial visibility, or manual finance work that no longer scales.

01

Accounting Cleanup

Reconcile balance-sheet accounts, correct historical errors, clean the general ledger, resolve unsupported balances, and establish a reliable starting point for ongoing accounting.

02

Fractional Controller

Senior accounting leadership without the fixed cost of a full-time Controller: close oversight, technical accounting, controls, reporting, audit support, and finance-team direction.

03

Accounting OS & Monthly Close

Build a durable close architecture with reconciliations, schedules, review checkpoints, responsibility matrices, recurring workflows, and reporting deadlines.

04

Cash Flow & Management Reporting

Improve visibility through cash forecasting, management reporting, budget-to-actual analysis, KPI design, working-capital review, and decision-ready financial information.

05

Finance Automation & Data Engineering

Automate repeatable accounting workflows and connect fragmented financial data using Excel, Power Query, Python, SQL, cloud data tools, APIs, and AI-assisted processes—with accounting judgment and controls built in.

Approach

Stabilize. Standardize. Automate.

Automation is downstream of accounting quality. Sensei Advisors first establishes financial integrity, then builds a repeatable operating cadence, and only then automates the work that should be automated.

  1. 01

    Diagnose

    Identify close bottlenecks, unreliable balances, broken handoffs, duplicate work, and reporting gaps.

  2. 02

    Clean

    Reconcile accounts, correct the ledger, document assumptions, and establish a defensible financial baseline.

  3. 03

    Close

    Build the monthly accounting cadence: tasks, schedules, controls, owners, reviews, and deadlines.

  4. 04

    Forecast

    Convert accurate accounting into cash visibility, operating insights, variance analysis, and management reporting.

  5. 05

    Automate

    Use finance systems, data pipelines, rules, and AI to reduce repetitive work while preserving review and control.
